Two Pillars
Pillar One
How to Think
Establishing situational awareness before first engagement contact. Know what archetype you are in before the first meeting. Understand what is absent as clearly as what is present. Prepare so thoroughly that calm becomes inevitable.
Pillar Two
How to Talk
Speak plainly to every room. Ask the question you already know the shape of. Never use jargon to seem credible. Hold the position without making it personal. Let the evidence speak. You just place it.
Who This Is For
Solution Architects
Engaged mid-program with no documentation, competing narratives, and a delivery deadline that doesn't move.
Enterprise Architects
Parachuted into troubled programs where previous attempts have failed and no one will say why.
Consulting Professionals
Navigating vendor-controlled environments where disclosure is managed and decisions are being made without you.
Technical Leads
Managing politically difficult stakeholder landscapes where technical clarity is the only lever available.
When to Use VAF·SA
+
Engagement is stalled and no one can clearly articulate why progress has stopped.
+
Vendor controls the information flow and full disclosure has not occurred.
+
Documentation is absent or incomplete — you are designing from verbal briefings and assumptions.
+
Political dynamics are preventing honest technical assessment from reaching decision-makers.
+
You have inherited an under-specified engagement with time pressure and no structured handover.
+
Clarity is required in weeks, not months — decision-makers need artefacts, not analysis.

Templates and Artefacts
CIS
Customer Impact Statement
One-page business case for technical decisions. Translates architecture risk into business language decision-makers can act on.
AoaP

The canonical context diagram. One page. No second page. Forces thinking to completion before committing.
HM

Forensic instrument for obfuscation engagements. Every blank cell is a finding. Pattern analysis across capability domains.

Four Engagement Archetypes
01
The Obfuscation Engagement
Information exists and is being deliberately withheld. The vendor has a vested interest in preventing full disclosure. The heat map is the forensic instrument. Every blank cell is evidence.
02
The Negligent Void
Nothing has been done. No documentation. No data classification. No vendor alignment. The engagement exists on paper. It has no architectural foundation.
03
The Institutional Paralysis
Multiple attempts. Different parties. No ownership. History withheld. Budget spent. Nothing delivered. Three months of immersion. Silence under pressure. Discharge the work.
04
The Silo Engagement
Capability exists. The solution is achievable. The parties who need to collaborate have never been in the same room. The workshop is the solution.
Four Operating Principles
I
No Process First
The framework is the vehicle. The outcome is the destination. Process never leads.
II
Decision Altitude
Every decision at the right level. Business altitude for business decisions. Technical altitude for technical decisions.
III
One Artefact
If it does not fit on one page, the thinking is not done. The second page is where incomplete thinking hides.
IV
Velocity Over Volume
Insight delivered fast beats documentation delivered late. Always.
