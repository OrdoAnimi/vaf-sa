Framework

What VAF·SA Is

VAF·SA is a practitioner framework for solution architects operating in complex, unclear, or politically difficult delivery environments. It provides the method — not just the models.

Most architecture frameworks assume the engagement is clean: documentation exists, stakeholders are cooperative, and the problem is well-defined. VAF·SA assumes the opposite. It was built for the environments architects actually encounter — stalled programs, vendor-controlled information flows, inherited engagements, and situations where producing the wrong artefact wastes everyone's time.

VAF·SA helps architects:

  • Understand the engagement context before producing anything
  • Identify missing information and diagnose why it is missing
  • Diagnose stakeholder conditions, delivery constraints, and risk
  • Select and shape the minimum useful architecture artefact for the situation
  • Communicate decisions clearly to every audience, at the right altitude
  • Avoid producing documents that do not change outcomes
Two Pillars

How Practitioners Operate

VAF·SA is organised around two foundational operating disciplines. Both must be active simultaneously.

Pillar One
Read the Environment

Diagnose the engagement before producing artefacts. Establish situational awareness before first engagement contact. Know which archetype you are in before the first meeting. Understand what is absent as clearly as what is present. The quality of your output is determined by the quality of your preparation.

Produce the Right Artefact
Pillar Two

Select and shape the minimum useful architecture artefact for the situation, audience, and decision. Speak plainly to every room. Never use jargon to seem credible. Hold the position without making it personal. Let the evidence speak. You just place it.

Engagement Diagnostics

Four Engagement Archetypes

Before producing any artefact, identify which archetype you are operating in. The archetype determines the correct practitioner response. Module 01 covers archetype identification in detail.

Archetype 01
The Obfuscation Engagement
Signal
Information exists but is being withheld. Vendor controls the disclosure timeline.
Meaning
Deliberate management of information flow to protect a position or vendor interest.
Response
Deploy the Heat Map. Every blank cell is a finding. Pattern analysis reveals what is being protected.
See
Module 02 — Intelligence →
Archetype 02
The Negligent Void
Signal
No documentation. No data classification. No vendor alignment. The engagement exists on paper only.
Meaning
The architectural foundation was never built. Nothing has been done, not merely withheld.
Response
Begin from zero. Structure the environment before attempting to design within it.
See
Module 03 — Design →
Archetype 03
The Institutional Paralysis
Signal
Multiple prior attempts, different parties, no ownership. Budget spent. History withheld. Nothing delivered.
Meaning
Organisational friction has prevented completion. The problem is structural, not technical.
Response
Three months of immersion. Silence under pressure. Discharge the work. Escalate when the boundary is reached.
See
Escalation Protocol →
Archetype 04
The Silo Engagement
Signal
Capability exists. The solution is achievable. The parties who need to collaborate have never shared a room.
Meaning
The block is structural and relational, not technical. The knowledge exists, but not in the same place.
Response
The workshop is the solution. Design the room. Manage the opening. Do not let the right answer leave without a commitment.

Templates and Artefacts

The Five Practitioner Artefacts

VAF·SA defines five minimum-useful artefacts. Each is designed to produce a decision, not a document.

CIS
Customer Impact Statement
One-page business case for technical decisions. Translates architecture risk into business language decision-makers can act on.
AoaP
Architecture on a Page
The canonical context diagram. One page. No second page. Forces thinking to completion before committing.
HM
Heat Map
Forensic instrument for obfuscation engagements. Every blank cell is a finding. Pattern analysis across capability domains.
ADR
Architecture Decision Record
Decision-ready documentation. Context, options considered, rationale, consequences. Audit-grade record of intent.
SCR
Stakeholder Concern Register
Formal capture of concerns, owners, and resolution status. Creates accountability without requiring formal authority.

Operating Principles

Four Operating Principles

I
No Process First
The framework is the vehicle. The outcome is the destination. Process never leads.
II
Decision Altitude
Every decision at the right level. Business altitude for business decisions. Technical altitude for technical decisions.
III
One Artefact
If it does not fit on one page, the thinking is not done. The second page is where incomplete thinking hides.
IV
Velocity Over Volume
Insight delivered fast beats documentation delivered late. Always.

About

VAF·SA is authored by Phil Myint, Global Enterprise Architect and founder of ZenCloud. The framework reflects practical experience across solution architecture, enterprise architecture, governance, integration, cloud transformation, and delivery environments where architecture must influence outcomes without formal authority.

VAF·SA was not developed as an academic exercise. It was built incrementally across engagements where conventional methods were insufficient — environments defined by unclear ownership, managed disclosure, institutional obstruction, and the absence of the conditions that most frameworks assume. The content is NDA-clean and reflects patterns, not specifics.
