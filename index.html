The six modules

From context to velocity.

OrdoAnimi Architecture is organised as a sequence of field moves, not a linear documentation method. Each module helps the architect reduce ambiguity and increase decision quality.

Module 01

Orientation

Read the engagement, mandate, politics, constraints, and delivery weather before prescribing architecture activity.

Module 02

Intelligence

Extract the missing business, technical, security, delivery, and stakeholder information needed to shape responsibly.

Module 03

Design

Translate context into solution options, boundaries, trade-offs, and implementation-ready decisions.

Module 04

Artefacts

Select the minimum useful artefact set and produce outputs that are fit for decision, governance, or delivery.

Module 05

Communication

Shape the narrative for executives, delivery teams, vendors, governance forums, and business stakeholders.

Module 06

Velocity Loop

Keep architecture alive through delivery by continuously aligning evidence, decisions, and action.

Field instruments

Practical tools for messy engagements.

Use these instruments when the work is blocked, unclear, misrepresented, undocumented, or moving faster than governance.

Context Scan

Quick read of mandate, power, risk, urgency, and stakeholder posture.

Intelligence Ledger

Track missing information, owner, evidence quality, and impact on decisions.

Decision Frame

Clarify the decision required, options, trade-offs, constraints, and recommendation.

Artefact Fit Check

Confirm whether an artefact will actually move governance or delivery forward.

Engagement archetypes

Know the terrain before choosing the artefact.

Obfuscation

When complexity, language, or politics hide the real issue.

Negligent Void

When documentation, ownership, and controls should exist but do not.

Institutional Paralysis

When decisions are known but cannot move through the system.

Silo Engagement

When local optimisation blocks enterprise-level architecture fitness.
