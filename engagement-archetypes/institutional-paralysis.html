Archetype 03

The Institutional Paralysis

The organisation has tried and failed. Multiple times.
Systemic Immersive Posture Module 01
The organisation has attempted this before. Multiple times. By multiple parties. No one will own it. History is being withheld or denied. Budget has been spent. Nothing has been delivered. The defining characteristic is accumulated inertia — each failed attempt adds political weight against the next one. You are not starting fresh. You are starting after everyone who came before you failed.
Posture
Immersive & Silent
Primary Risk
Political elimination before delivery
Velocity Target
Up to 30 days to CIS
Detection

Signals

Observable Signals
  • "We haven't really started yet" — 18+ months in
  • No handover between previous attempts
  • Documents exist but are not being shared
  • Pressure applied before delivery begins
  • $10k+/month spend with nothing to show
  • Multiple teams involved, none completed
  • Sponsor changes frequently or is absent

Operating Posture

Immersive and Silent

Field Instruction
Three months of ingestion if the environment requires it. Read everything. Say nothing under pressure. Discharge the work. Do not complain. Do not fight. Deliver. The Institutional Paralysis is won by producing artefacts that cannot be ignored — not by winning arguments about why previous attempts failed.

In the Institutional Paralysis, you carry the political weight of every prior failure. The organisation has built a reflex against this work. Engaging with it directly will trigger it.

Your protection is output. A signed artefact is politically harder to reject than a conversation. Produce before you argue.

Field Execution

Tactical Guidance

The pressure you receive at the start of this engagement is a test of whether you will behave like every other engagement that failed. Absorb it. Do not react. The organisation is watching to see if you are different.

Find the failure history. Every prior attempt left traces — presentations, emails, a disengaged consultant who will talk. Understand why they failed before you design your approach. You are inheriting their mistakes.

Do not inherit their framing. Previous attempts may have been positioned incorrectly or scoped wrong. You are allowed to reframe. Present the problem differently than it was presented before.

Identify the one person who wants this to succeed. In every Institutional Paralysis there is one person with a legitimate stake in delivery. Find them. They are your distribution channel for the first artefact.

Combination Patterns

When Archetypes Combine

Common combinations with Institutional Paralysis:

Paralysis + Obfuscation
Prior failures are being hidden and you cannot access the history. The obfuscation is protecting the organisation from accountability for past spend. Operate forensically within an immersive posture.
Paralysis + Negligent Void
Years of failed attempts AND no foundation was ever built. The most time-intensive combination. Extended ingestion period is non-negotiable — do not attempt to compress it.
